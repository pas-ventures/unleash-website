Venture
studio

Unleash Ventures is the South African venture studio of Philipp Ströhemann's family office. We support South African founders from early-stage incubation to pre-seed with strategic mentorship and resources, and with capital. We can act as your lead investor, and also as a co-founder.

Watch: we build camels, not unicorns →

Why we build
camels

For years, South African founders and investors have been sold the same story: raise a pre-seed early at a high valuation, paint a pan-African vision, and the next rounds will come. Follow-on rounds rarely materialise. South Africa has hardly any exit market for startups, and founders and early investors get diluted at every step.

That model was built for the United States, the largest and most liquid market in the world. Here the maths does not add up. So we build camels instead: cash-flow-driven businesses, resilient in the South African context, that give the founder the flexibility to succeed.
