Weekly Tracking

The progress board — how it works

One shared sheet. Every founder has a row. Every Friday, you fill in your line.

  • It's the tell. We've never invested in or mentored a founder who wasn't tracking their progress. It's a core skill.
  • Two things a week: your goal, and your current blockers / help needed.
  • Green means you posted. The board makes it obvious who's executing.
  • We read it before every Q&A — activity earns airtime. The most active get the most of Phil's time.

Do It Right

Sharp goals. Specific blockers.

Goals = SMART
  • Specific · Measurable · Achievable · Relevant · Time-bound.
  • ✗ Weak: "Finish my strategy." Not specific — what is even done?
  • ✓ Strong: "Strategy doc finished and reviewed with my 3 co-founders, ready to take to market next week."
If we can't measure it, we can't help you hit it.
Blockers = be specific
  • Goal — what you're going for.
  • Reality — what's actually happening right now.
  • Blocker — what you've tried that isn't working.
  • The ask — exactly what you need, and from whom: the community, or Unleash.
The clearer the ask, the faster we get the right resource or intro in front of you.
