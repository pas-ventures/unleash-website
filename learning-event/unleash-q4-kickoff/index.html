02

South Africa has the entrepreneurial power, but not the funding mechanisms to match it.

1
  • Since 2024 we've spoken to hundreds of founders.
  • The common belief: you need investment to get your company started. That isn't true.
  • VC is right for some businesses, not for most — not even in the US or Europe.
  • South Africa needs different forms of funding, not VC dependency.
2
  • The next wave of AI businesses in South Africa gets funded very differently.
  • That's what Unleash Ventures believes in:
  • Lean building.
  • Cash-flow positive within 24 months.
  • A real South African problem first, then expand — not a pan-African picture with no evidence behind it.
3

The result we expect for founders: more optionality, more control over the business, more equity to keep, and more flexibility in uncertain terrain.

More context: The VC Illusion: Why SA Venture Capital Has Failed Founders

Unleash Ventures: a new studio approach for South Africa

One

The Unleash Community

What you've just joined. Free to access and invite-only, for early-stage founders.

The aim: to become the go-to partner for early-stage founders in South Africa.

Two

Unleash Ventures, the investing arm

A studio that doesn't take 50%. We work with high optionality — the funding and equity structure your business actually needs.

We invest up to R3 million of our own money, and can top up to R10 million with angels we bring in.

We act as a shadow co-founder — product, go-to-market, the right team. Whatever it takes, we get our hands dirty.

How to get the most out of it: agency and proactivity

Stay active
  • Watch this space and come to the calls.
  • Bring your questions. Come prepared.
  • Work with the progress board every week.
  • Help other people out. It's how the room — and we — get to see how you work.
Be proactive

We're not an accelerator that runs you through a programme and calls you fundable at the end. That isn't how it works. What works is founders empowering themselves — getting the right information at the right time, and working with the community and with us to get their results.

The venture assessment, and your one-to-one

They replace the pitch deck. Instead of sending a polished deck out into silence, we get value back to you — through the same studio toolset we use on our own companies.

The venture assessment
  • You're fully in control of what you share.
  • It's text, so the feedback comes back as text — far easier to work with in your own AI tools.
  • Written out, it carries much more than slides do, and it lets us have a proper conversation.
Your one-to-one
  • A feel for what working with Unleash is actually like — how we think and how we go about things.
  • We learn your business properly.
  • It can be about investment. It can be sparring. It can be something else entirely.
  • Make it yours, and come with your questions.

Most common questions

Will Unleash Ventures invest in my business?
If it's the right fit, we'd love to. Full disclosure: we invest in two to three companies a year, so most of the time the answer is no — and you still get the feedback and, where we can, introductions to the right next step. You get value either way.
Why do you spend so much time on this?
Because it's the job. Seeing businesses, meeting founders and building relationships is what any investor does — we just do it in a way that's useful to the community. Instead of networking, a value exchange.
What do you get out of it?
We see a lot of ventures, so we can make the connections and pay it forward. And we get to meet the people we want to do business with for the rest of our lives.
Will you introduce me to angels and advisors?
We do our best, but we can't promise it. When we see a fit, we share you with our network — send us a one-pager and we'll pass it on and ask them to reach out. If you don't hear back, it wasn't relevant enough for them. And the right moment rarely lands inside one cohort: we meet in October, and the right investor turns up next May. So keep in touch and keep us updated — that's how it actually works.
How do I connect with other founders?
The WhatsApp group and the platform, where members are sorted by city. Nicolene also matches founders directly.
How can I trust you?
Don't take our word for it. Take the references, speak to people in our network, and ask other members what it's actually been like.
