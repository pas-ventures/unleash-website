Why we're here

Most founders in South Africa spend their time preparing for a round that never comes.
  • Most businesses here are not directly fundable by venture capital — and plenty of good ones need a different kind of funding altogether.
  • What you actually want is optionality: a lean operation, customer results, revenue, and the ability to raise from a position of strength, if you raise at all.
  • And our side of it, plainly: we're growing our investment portfolio, and this is where we meet the founders we want to back. We make our money in the long run through investments, not from this community.

What Unleash offers

We also look at your business alongside everything else we see, and tell you honestly where it stands.

The cheque

R1M to R3M (€50k–150k) into businesses we believe can reach cash-flow positive in 18 to 24 months.

Alongside us

We bring in strategic angels and top-up funding where a round needs more than we put in.

The terms

The same share class as you. No preferences, no special rights — we dilute when you dilute, so everyone is incentivised the same way.

The bench

Our network, the right company structure, the lawyer bench, the go-to-market and tech repositories, and co-founder scouting from our database.

And the operators to push it forward — product, marketing and sales.

How to get the most out of it

Use it, your way
  • Nothing here is mandatory. Find the way of working with it that suits you.
  • Show up for the calls and bring your questions. Come prepared.
  • Keep your weekly progress updated.
How we show up
  • Keep it confidential. People share openly here and we expect everyone to honour that.
  • No pitching, no spam. We want to hear about your venture — keep side offers out of it.
  • Help where you can.
Be proactive

Unleash is not responsible for your success. We're not an accelerator that runs you through a programme and hands you a fundable startup at the end. The founders who are most proactive get the most of our time, and they're the ones this community gets to know.

We ran quarters. The community is bigger now, there are more ventures to get through, and a quarter that ends in January runs straight into the South African holidays. So this one is four months — so we don't lose the time, and everyone gets the attention.

The progress board

The single most important habit here. One row per founder, three columns a week.

  • Goal for the week — what you're actually going after.
  • Current blockers and help needed — specific enough that someone can act on it.
  • Win and learning — what moved, and what you now know.
  • We read it and reply in the sheet, so you get unblocked weekly.
  • It shapes what the Q&A calls cover — and the call schedule lives on it too, in a tab called Q&A sessions.

The venture assessment, and your one-to-one

Eleven sections — snapshot, founders, customer and problem, product, market, traction, economics, go-to-market, roadmap, fundraising, risks. Everyone who submits one gets written feedback. Questions after that go on the progress board.

The session is yours, and it's not a pitch
  • It can be sparring until you're investment-ready, whoever ends up investing.
  • It can be a partnership — us looking seriously at investing, and what that would take.
  • It can be no raise at all, and we work on the business instead.
Who we meet in person
  • We can't meet everyone. We prioritise early ventures with a real customer problem and a market worth going after.
  • And the founders our network should meet — strongest traction, or a space we find genuinely interesting.
  • Last quarter we met around 70% of founders. This cohort is bigger, so it may take longer.

The Q&A calls

  • At least every two weeks — more when something urgent comes up that's worth everyone's time.
  • Tuesdays and Thursdays, between 11:00 and 14:00. Over lunchtime, so it fits around a working day.
  • One to one and a half hours, depending on how much demand there is on the day.
  • Scheduled two to four weeks ahead. You'll be invited, so watch that space.
  • Your questions answered directly, or the room answering each other — sometimes with mentors and senior founders in with us. It's interactive.

Questions we get a lot

1
What can I expect this quarter?
October to get acquainted and onto the board. Assessment in by 1 November. One-to-ones from end of October. November and December to execute, and we decide together in January.
2
How do I use the progress board?
One row a week, on a Friday. Your goal, your blockers, your win and learning. We reply in the sheet, and it shapes what the calls cover.
3
When can I expect a one-to-one with Phil?
Between mid-October and mid-December. First come, first served — so it's worth starting the assessment in detail now.
4
How often do you invest?
Two to three a year, at R1M to R3M, with strategic angels alongside where a round needs more.
5
What if you don't invest — what happens then?
That's the normal outcome, and it isn't a failure. You keep the written feedback, the community, the calls and the introductions — and we tell you what we see in our network for you.
