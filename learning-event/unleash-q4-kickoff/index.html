01

Why we're here

  • Our goal is to get South African entrepreneurship unleashed. This community is how we do it.
  • Actionable feedback for founders on the ground, from people who have actually built and sold companies — through a community you can reach from anywhere.
  • And the second reason, straight: we're growing our investment portfolio, and this is where we meet the founders we want to back.
02

Who's in the room

For the first time, more than 50 of you are joining in one go — alongside the founders who've been here since July.

  • We still read every application ourselves, and we stay selective.
  • A bigger group means more founders helping each other — more people to learn from, and more founders to be matched with.
  • The attention you get from us still follows the work you put in.
You're early — pre-revenue, or a handful of design partners, mostly under R2M on the new venture. Open to feedback, and still working out what kind of investment, if any, to take.
03

What you get

  • A WhatsApp group of founders at the same stage as you.
  • Q&A calls, shaped around the real problems coming up in the group.
  • One-on-one sparring with Phil for the ventures that fit — founders building camels, not unicorns.
  • A members section with everything we learn on the calls, plus the AI tools we've built.
  • Templates for the hard parts — validating your idea, going to market, building your deck.
All of it free.
04

What we ask of you

Do the work
  • Fill out the assessment if you want feedback on your venture.
  • Show up for the calls and bring your questions. Come prepared.
  • Keep your weekly progress updated — it shows us what to prioritise next.
How we show up
  • Keep it confidential. People share openly here and we expect everyone to honour that.
  • No pitching, no spam. We want to hear about your venture — keep side offers out of it.
  • Use the group, not DMs, so the answer helps everyone.
05

The trimester: October to end of January

Month 1

Get started

Get acquainted with the documents, join a Q&A, start on the progress board, and fill out the venture assessment.

Between 1 and 2

The assessment

We read every one and send written feedback. One-to-one sessions run from the end of October.

Months 2–3

Execute

You've got feedback and two or three things worth focusing on. We work through them together — on the calls, in the group, on the board.

Month 4

Decide

Where you got to, and what's still blocking. Then we decide together: keep working one-on-one, or a warm introduction onward.

Four months, not three. December stays open for anyone who wants to keep going, and the progress board stays live throughout.
06

Month one

  1. Get acquainted with the documents. The guidelines, the members section, the tools.
  2. Join a Q&A call. You don't need to have done anything first.
  3. Start on the progress board. This is the important one.
  4. Fill out the venture assessment. It's more work than most people expect, so start straight away.
The board and the assessment are the two that matter. Submit the assessment by 1 November.
07

The progress board

One row per founder. Three columns a week. Fill it in on a Friday.

  • Goal for the week — what you're actually going after.
  • Current blockers and help needed — specific enough that someone can act on it.
  • Win and learning — what moved, and what you now know.
  • We read it and reply in the sheet. It shapes what the Q&A calls cover.
  • It also carries the Q&A sessions tab — the schedule lives there.

08

The venture assessment

Eleven sections: snapshot · founders · customer and problem · product · market · traction · economics · GTM · roadmap · fundraising · risks.

What it is

The thinking, not the form

More work than most people expect. That's the point — the eleven sections force what you'd otherwise skip.

What comes back

Short and direct

We read every one. Written feedback, shorter and sharper this quarter — the few things to act on, and where this model has worked before.

Then

The document stays yours

After your session we add our notes to it and share it back with you.

Submit by 1 November. One-to-one sessions run from the end of October.
09

The one-to-one session

The session is about what you bring. Come with your current topics — the ones that matter most right now.

Prepare what you want out of it
  • Feedback on the venture.
  • Asking for investment.
  • Introductions — and name who.
And what we bring
  • A worked session on what needs to happen next for the business — decided together.
  • The Unleash Ventures read: what we would need to see to take the collaboration further, or to invest.
10

The venture one-pager, and the advisor message

Tool one

A one-pager with your main thesis

The page you send when someone says “send me something short”. It reads your finished assessment, tells you what an investor would find missing, then builds a one-page PDF in your own brand — plus the message that carries it.

Tool two

A message to reach potential advisors

Outreach to advisors, operators and angels that leads with something you've published or built, never with a pitch.

Both run on the finished assessment.
11

Q&A calls and guest speakers

The calls
  • 90 minutes now, so there's room for real problems.
  • At least every two weeks, more often if needed.
  • Usually Tuesdays and Thursdays, 11:00–14:00. Block the window.
  • Dates live in the Q&A sessions tab on the progress board.
  • Bring a question. Sign up for the hot seat — it feels challenging, and every founder who's done it was glad they did.
Guest speakers
  • We're building a bench of operators and investors for the calls.
  • Members run sessions too — if you've learned something worth sharing, say so.
  • No pitches. Value first, every time.
  • Someone you'd love to hear from, or can introduce us to? Tell Nicolene.
12

You keep everything

  • You keep everything. The board, the calls, the tools, the group — none of it resets.
  • A bigger room means more people to learn from and more founders to be matched with.
  • Made real progress? Put it on the progress board. That's how it reaches us.
  • The one thing we ask to keep your seat: fill in the quarterly survey. It's how we decide what to change.
