The Core Step

The venture assessment

Eleven sections: snapshot · founders · customer and problem · product · market · traction · economics · GTM · roadmap · fundraising · risks.

  • It's more work than most people expect. The eleven sections force the thinking you'd otherwise skip.
  • We read every one. You get written feedback — shorter and sharper this quarter, three points that matter rather than thirty.
  • The feedback now also covers where this model has worked before, and why — and where the VC path, the bootstrap path and the Unleash path actually lead.
  • Where there's a fit, we book a one-to-one.
Submit by 1 November. Feedback and one-to-ones come back over the course of the trimester — realistically from December into January, given the holidays. If we're faster, even better.
New This Trimester

Two new tools

Most founders last cohort couldn't write either of these. So we built them — and we'll start both in this session.

Tool one

The venture one-pager

The page you send when someone says “send me something short”. It reads your finished assessment, tells you what an investor would find missing, then builds a one-page PDF in your own brand — plus the message that carries it.

Tool two

The advisor outreach message

Content-first outreach to advisors, operators and angels. It leads with something you've published or built. Never with a pitch.

Both run on the finished assessment — that's why it comes first.

Exercise · 6 minutes

The advisor message

Four lines. One volunteer, rewritten live.

  1. The thing they published or built that you actually read. Specific enough that it couldn't be sent to anyone else.
  2. The one sentence about you that earns the next line. What you've built, what's working.
  3. The specific ask. Not “pick your brain” — a question only they can answer, or a named introduction.
  4. The easy out. Make no a comfortable answer, and you'll get more yeses.
