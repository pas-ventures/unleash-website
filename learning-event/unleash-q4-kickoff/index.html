What if South African founders built revenue-driven businesses instead of opting into the VC race?

  • After speaking to hundreds of founders, the same pattern keeps coming up: the sense that you need an investor before you can really start building. Then the time turns out to have been better spent with customers.
  • Venture capital is a real path and a good one for some. It just isn't the reality for every business — and building as though it is costs you the years you could have been earning.
  • We think the next wave of AI businesses in South Africa gets funded differently. That's what Unleash Ventures and this community are for.
  • What we want to give you: optionality · the freedom to build without depending on investors · and the room to build something that actually pays you.

What Unleash is

One

The Unleash Community

Where you are right now. A free community for early-stage South African founders.

It helps you understand where you actually are, and what step to take next — and it's how we work out whether Unleash invests or not.

Two

Unleash Ventures

Phil's venture investment arm for South Africa, run from a family office working across Europe and South Africa.

We incubate and start two to three companies a year. An AI-enabled studio model with the funding behind it — up to R3M from us, more from angels alongside.

Hands-on: incubation, early traction, the right first customers, the product, co-founders where they're needed, and the right advisors.

How to get the most out of it

Use it, your way
  • Nothing here is mandatory. Find the way of working with it that suits you.
  • Show up for the calls and bring your questions. Come prepared.
  • Keep your weekly progress updated.
How we show up
  • Keep it confidential. People share openly here and we expect everyone to honour that.
  • No pitching, no spam. We want to hear about your venture — keep side offers out of it.
  • Help where you can.
Be proactive

Unleash is not responsible for your success. We're not an accelerator that runs you through a programme and hands you a fundable startup at the end. The founders who are most proactive get the most of our time, and they're the ones this community gets to know.

08

Unleash works in cohorts

One cohort per trimester, and this one runs October to January. Cohorts give everyone the same focus and proper sprints, instead of everybody drifting at their own pace. The cohort is your anchor — you stay in the community as long as you like.

1
October

Getting started

Into the group, onto the progress board, and into the assessment. Tell us what you're stuck with, so we start understanding your business.

2
To 1 November

Assessing where you are

The venture assessment, written feedback on it, and your one-to-one. An honest read on where the business actually stands.

3
November – December

Executing together

The two or three things worth focusing on — worked through on the calls, in the group and on the board.

4
January

A proper decision

What the next path looks like for you: Unleash as a sparring partner, Unleash investing, or a different kind of investor and different advisors. We help you make that call.

The venture assessment, and your one-to-one

Together they replace the pitch deck. The point is to get you as much value as fast as possible.

The venture assessment
  • You're fully in control of what you share.
  • It's text, so the feedback comes back as text — far easier to work with in your own AI tools.
  • Written out, it carries much more than slides do, and it lets us have a proper conversation.
Your one-to-one
  • A feel for what working with Unleash is actually like — how we think and how we go about things.
  • We learn your business properly.
  • It can be about investment. It can be sparring. It can be something else entirely.
  • Make it yours, and come with your questions.

Most common questions

What can I expect this quarter?
October to get acquainted and onto the board. Assessment in by 1 November, then your one-to-one. Execute through to December, and we decide together in January.
Will Unleash Ventures invest in my business?
Most likely not — we do two to three a year. If it's a fit we'll say so early, and tell you what we'd need to see.
What can I expect if you don't invest?
The thing most people come for anyway: written feedback, the calls, the community and introductions. And a straight read on what we see in our network for you.
How do I connect with other founders?
The WhatsApp group and the platform, where members are sorted by city. Nicolene also matches founders directly.
Will you introduce me to angels and advisors?
Yes, when we can be useful. Tell us who and why — named asks get acted on, “intro me to investors” doesn't.
How do I know my idea won't be stolen?
Everything here is confidential and we expect every member to honour that. Share only what you're comfortable sharing.
How can I trust you?
Don't take our word for it. Take the references, speak to people in our network, and ask other members what it's actually been like.
