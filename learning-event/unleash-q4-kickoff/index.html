01

Why we're here

Most founders in South Africa spend their time preparing for a round that never comes.
  • Most businesses here are not directly fundable by venture capital. There are outliers, but building as if you are one is how good companies stall.
  • What you actually want is optionality — and plenty of interesting businesses need a different kind of funding altogether. That's where Unleash was born, and it's what we work out together with you.
  • So you build differently: a lean operation, customer results, revenue — and the ability to raise from a position of strength, if you raise at all.

The progress board

The single most important habit here. One row per founder, three columns a week.

  • Goal for the week — what you're actually going after.
  • Current blockers and help needed — specific enough that someone can act on it.
  • Win and learning — what moved, and what you now know.
  • We read it and reply in the sheet, so you get unblocked weekly.
  • It shapes what the Q&A calls cover, and it carries the Q&A sessions tab.

06

What we ask of you: get active

Use it, your way
  • Nothing here is mandatory. Find the way of working with it that suits you — the calls, the group, the board, the assessment.
  • Show up for the calls and bring your questions. Come prepared.
  • Keep your weekly progress updated.
How we show up
  • Keep it confidential. People share openly here and we expect everyone to honour that.
  • No pitching, no spam. We want to hear about your venture — keep side offers out of it.
  • Help where you can.
The one thing to take from this

Unleash is not responsible for your success. We're not an accelerator that runs you through a programme and hands you a fundable startup at the end — that isn't how it works. The founders who are most proactive get the most of our time, and they're the ones this community gets to know. Give and take.

We ran quarters. The community is bigger now, there are more ventures to get through, and a quarter that ends in January runs straight into the South African holidays. So this one is four months — so we don't lose the time, and everyone gets the attention.

08

The venture assessment, and your one-to-one

Eleven sections — snapshot, founders, customer and problem, product, market, traction, economics, GTM, roadmap, fundraising, risks. Everyone who submits one gets written feedback. Further questions after that go on the progress board.

The session is yours, and it's not a pitch
  • It can be sparring until you're investment-ready, whoever ends up investing.
  • It can be a partnership — us looking seriously at investing, and what that would take.
  • It can be no raise at all, and we work on the business instead.
Who we meet in person
  • We can't meet everyone. We prioritise early ventures with a real customer problem and a market worth going after, where we could become a partner.
  • And the founders our network should meet — strongest traction, or a space we find genuinely interesting.
  • Last quarter we met around 70% of founders. This cohort is bigger, so it may take longer.
09

The Q&A calls

How they run
  • At least every two weeks — more when something urgent comes up that's worth everyone's time.
  • Tuesdays and Thursdays, 11:00 or 13:00. You'll be invited, so watch that space.
  • Scheduled two to four weeks ahead, so we can move with what's actually going on.
  • Your questions answered directly, or the room answering each other — sometimes with mentors and senior founders in with us. It's interactive.
What a session can be
  • The hot seat. A founder volunteers and gets questioned — like a venture assessment, but shorter. Tell us if you're up for it.
  • A guest speaker with real depth on one topic.
  • A founder from here with learnings worth sharing, from their time at Unleash or from before it.
  • Someone you want to hear from, or something you want to present — tell Nicolene.

11

Questions we get a lot

When can I expect a one-to-one with Phil?
Between mid-October and mid-December. First come, first served — so it's worth starting your assessment in detail now.
What if my assessment isn't good enough?
If it isn't comprehensive enough for us to have a proper session, we'll tell you what's missing rather than sit on it.
Can I use AI to write it?
Yes, we expect you to. But if it reads as slop that doesn't hold together, we'll say so — we can't run a session off it.
What if I don't want a session at all?
Then use the assessment for yourself. Founders keep telling us that's where most of the value was.
