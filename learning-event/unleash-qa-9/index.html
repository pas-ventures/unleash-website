Why We're Here

What is Unleash?

A venture-building initiative — not an accelerator — built to scale the learnings of dozens of former operators into the founders who need them. South Africa needs more companies, not more pitch decks chasing a VC hype cycle that doesn't work here. Our aim: make the SA tech ecosystem investable, and build cash-flow-driven, profitable companies riding the next wave.

Initiated by Philipp Ströhemann — co-founded & bootstrapped ComX to €18M ARR, exit to PE in 2022 — with dozens of operators & investors alongside.
Value first
Everyone leaves this quarter with something valuable — whether we invest or not. We measure it.
Operators only
Feedback from operators and investors with skin in the game. No service-sellers, no consultants with a pitch deck.
Camels over unicorns
Cash-flow-positive businesses that grow independent of funding cycles. We'll still back the right VC fit.

Community Guidelines

How we show up

🚫

No pitching

Show up to move your business forward, not to pitch.

🔕

No spam

No selling to the group, no cold DMs to members. Keep the community clean.

🤝

Help where you can

Answer, intro, share what worked. Speak from experience, be open.

⚡

Stay active

Engage fully. Put in the effort, and Unleash gives it back.

🦸

Attitude: positive Power Rangers

See something that could add more value for the community? Tell us — constructively. Baseless complaints go nowhere; a sharp, specific idea that lifts everyone, we act on. That's the deal.

The Whole Game

Three things that need your action

Do all three and you get the value — even if it takes longer for some of you. Stick with it.

1

Submit your assessment

Log in and submit it this month. Without your context we can't give real feedback — the Q&As help, but pitch decks don't cut it. That's what gets you a personal review and an investment-fit check.

2

Track your weekly progress

Every Friday: your goal, your current blockers / help needed. Voluntary — but the founders who track get the most from us. Need an intro? Put it there.

3

Show up for the Q&As

Use Slido, engage, tell us where you're stuck. Think about what you actually need — then bring it.

Goals = SMART: Specific · Measurable · Actionable · Relevant · Time-bound. No fluff — a goal we can actually help you hit.
