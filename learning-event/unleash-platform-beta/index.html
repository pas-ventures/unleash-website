Four phases and what you have at the end of each

Phase 1 · Problem confirmationAt least 10 transcribed interviews with your target group.
Phase 2 · First paying customers2 to 3 signed design partners and a paid pilot.
Phase 3 · Value delivery and case studies5 to 10 design partners turned into paying customers, with video testimonials.
Phase 4 · Make this a businessYour eleven-point framework from the venture assessment, and your round if you choose to raise.
Unleash.

What an exercise looks like

Each exercise has five parts:

  • What you’ll have at the end
  • Why it matters
  • The steps
  • Where to write it down
  • Which Q&A clip or tool helps

If you’re stuck, write what’s in the way and the app shows you the Q&A clips and tools that help.
