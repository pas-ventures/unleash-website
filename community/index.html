Community
& support

A free, application-only community built for the South African reality. Get help with the blockers in front of you today and find out whether you are investable. Talk to people who are already at the stage you want to reach, and get mentorship and advice when you need it. Where there is a fit, we introduce members to investors, including international ones.

What members
get

Every quarter

The Venture Assessment

An eleven-point questionnaire on the state of your business. The whole Unleash team reviews it and sends you feedback to act on.

Every two weeks

Q&A calls

Mentors from South Africa and abroad share what they learned. Two founders take the hot seat, thirty minutes each, and work through one live problem with the room.

Always on

The founder peer group

Founders at the same stage, in the same WhatsApp group, plus introductions to founders in your space.

When you need it

Mentor matching

Matched by sector and stage with operators who have built and sold companies.

Ongoing

The founder platform

Playbooks, spreadsheets, Claude skills and the progress board, in the Unleash app.

When it matters

One-to-one calls

Time with Phil and the team on the decision in front of you: raise now, from whom, or keep building.
