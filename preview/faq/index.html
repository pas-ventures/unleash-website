How Unleash works

The things founders actually ask us, answered properly. If something here is still unclear, email Nicolene and she will come back to you.

01 — Start here

What is Unleash?

A community of early-stage South African founders, run by operators who have built and sold their own companies and who invest their own money.

It has four parts: a WhatsApp community you join by applying, open Q&A calls anyone can attend, a three-month programme built around a venture assessment, and an investment studio that backs a small number of the founders who come through.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no fee to apply, to join the community, to attend the Q&As, or to run the three-month programme.

It is free because it is how we meet founders. We invest in a small number of the companies we get to know here, and seeing many ventures properly is worth more to us than a membership fee would be. You are not obliged to take investment, and most founders never discuss it with us.

Do you take equity for joining?

No. Joining the community costs you nothing and gives us nothing. No equity, no fee, no option, no right of first refusal.

If we later invest, that is a separate conversation with its own terms, and you are free to say no and stay in the community either way.

Is this an accelerator?

No, and the difference matters. Most accelerators are built around getting you funding-ready: polishing the deck, rehearsing the pitch, ending in a demo day. That is a good way to look investable and a poor way to become a real business.

We work on the business itself, and most of our feedback tells founders to narrow down and do less. There is no demo day. Founders who have been through both put it better than we can:

“So many other accelerators, incubators, venture studios all focus on pitch polishing and making sure your business is venture-ready and funding-ready, as opposed to building a company that’s sustainable.” — Yazid Bohardien, LaunchPath

02 — Are we a fit

Who is it for, and who is it not for?

Early-stage founders building in South Africa, from pre-revenue up to roughly R2M a year. Beyond that we will still look, and we may well meet you in person, but the programme is built for the earlier end.

It works best if you have a real problem and a real customer, you want to get the model right before you raise, and you are willing to be told things you would rather not hear. It works badly if you want applause.

How do I get in?

There are three ways in, and they are different sizes.

  • The Q&A calls — just turn up with a question. No application.
  • The WhatsApp community — apply, and sign up to the platform once you are accepted.
  • A one-to-one — be in a cohort and have completed your Venture Assessment.

When does the next cohort start?

January. We read applications year-round, so there is no closed window and no reason to wait.

If you are accepted before January you do not sit and wait. You go into the WhatsApp community, you get the platform and the playbooks, and you can come to every Q&A between now and the start.

03 — The three months

What is the Venture Assessment?

It is where every cohort starts, and it is the most useful thing we give you.

You spend about twenty-five minutes answering questions from our AI — the same questions an investor works through before writing a deal memo, drawn from what Phil has learned building and backing companies. It writes your venture up across eleven lenses and names the blockers standing between you and where you want to be.

Then Nicolene and Phil read it. Every single one, by hand. You get an honest read on whether there is a fit with what Unleash backs, and if there is not, we say why and point you somewhere better.

The write-up is yours. Most founders end up using it as the clearest description of their business they have ever had.

What happens over the three months?

  • Month one, assess. You do the Venture Assessment. We read it and come back to you. If it is a fit, you sit down with Phil and agree the three things that matter most.
  • Month two, execute. No new task. You work those three things, use the Q&As when you get stuck, and post your progress each week.
  • Month three, decide. We work out the right next step together, including whether you should be raising at all. Either we build with you, or we introduce you to someone better suited.

What do you expect from me?

Fill in the quarterly survey, and keep your weekly progress updated on the board. That is the whole obligation.

The work itself is yours to do. We tell you what we see; you decide what to do with it. Founders who post their progress get the most out of this, because that board is how we know who to help and when.

“You shouldn’t apply if you’re not actually going to fill in the board and you’re not going to participate. You only get out what you’re putting in.” — Anna Insam, Kleo Health

What happens if I don’t do that?

We rotate you out at the end of the quarter. Nothing personal, and you are welcome to come back.

We keep it because it is what makes the room worth being in. A community where nobody has to show up becomes a mailing list.

04 — Working with us

Do I get time with Phil one-on-one?

Yes, and it is earned. You need to be in a cohort and to have completed your Venture Assessment, because otherwise the hour gets spent on questions the assessment already answers.

It is the one thing here we have to ration. Everything else — the Q&As, the playbooks, the community, the assessment feedback — is open to everyone.

Do you invest? On what terms?

We do, in a small number of companies a year, with our own money. There is no fund, no outside investors, and nobody pushing us toward an exit on someone else’s timetable.

Where we invest we take meaningful equity at an early-stage valuation, because we build alongside you rather than sitting on a board twice a year. We would rather tell you that now than have you discover it late.

What if you don’t invest in me?

That is the normal outcome, and it costs you nothing.

You keep the assessment, the write-up, the frameworks and the community. We tell you plainly why we are not the right backer, and where we think you should look instead. The promise is that you get real value; it was never that we would write a cheque.

05 — Practical

Can I come to a Q&A without applying?

Yes. The Q&A calls run every two weeks and anyone can join. No application, no cost, no catch.

Bring one real question about your business. Two founders each session also take the hot seat — thirty minutes each, one live problem, worked through with the room.

Email Nicolene and she will send you the next date.

Do I have to be in Cape Town?

No. Everything runs online, and the community is spread across the country. We meet founders in person when we can, but nothing you need depends on it.

Who runs it?

Phil and Nicolene.

Phil co-founded ComX, bootstrapped it through Covid to more than 150 people and sold it to private equity. He now invests his own capital into founders in South Africa and Germany. Nicolene runs the community day to day and is who most founders speak to first.

Around them is a group of operators who have been through the same thing and give time to founders coming up behind them.

What happens to what I share?

What is said in the community stays in the community. Your assessment is stored securely, used only to give you feedback and to help us understand where you are, and deleted if you ask us to.

We never share your numbers with anyone outside the team without asking you first. The detail is in our privacy policy.

Most of the strongest founders here arrived because somebody already in the room vouched for them.
