Founder stories, and what changed

South African founders who came through Unleash, telling it themselves.

A big part of our mission is to tell these stories, so that fewer founders get stuck on the same things. There are people out there who have already walked the path. No founder should have to repeat every mistake that has already been made — the job is to get the feedback early. Below is what changed once these founders got the right information at the right time.
