What is Unleash

The studio, and the community around it

Unleash Ventures is the South African venture studio of Philipp Ströhemann’s family office. We incubate and invest in early-stage South African ventures built and backed by exceptional founders. We believe in founder-led businesses and founder control. We work alongside venture capital and other family offices, and we invest our own money at the right time to make founders successful.

We want to meet as many founders as we can, and the Unleash Community is how we do it. It is the best way to give real value to early-stage founders without running an accelerator, and without asking anyone to produce a pitch deck they never get feedback on. It is also how we bring exited entrepreneurs and mentors in, to help build the entrepreneurship in South Africa we all want.

Philipp Ströhemann bootstrapped and scaled his own company to a private-equity exit — 150+ people and €6M+ EBIT — and now helps founders in Europe and South Africa build towards the same kind of story.

If you want to meet Phil, or you are thinking about raising, apply and come and meet us. We would love to learn what you are building.

Who this is for

South African founders who have identified a real problem

Early-stage founders building in South Africa and beyond. A good fit usually looks like the following.

Early-stage

Pre-revenue, or around R2M in revenue at most.

A real problem and a real customer

You have identified both, and you can name them.

Hungry for hard feedback

You want it constructive and you want it straight.

Building lean before you raise

You build around the customer problem first, and raise after.

Looking for a mentor or funding

Or both, and you are ready to use them.

Open to outside eyes

You would rather hear it from someone outside the building.

After the people who move the lever

You want the few conversations that actually change the trajectory.

How it works

Unleash is application-only

Real value comes from people who show commitment. We only accept founders who meet the criteria — a real problem identified, early stage, and a willingness to learn. That is what makes being in the community valuable on its own: you land in a peer group of people who want to grow together.
1

You apply

Applications are open all year. Tell us what you are building and where you are.

2

The team reads it and comes back to you

Every application is attended to by a person, and we come back to you either way.

3

You are invited into the community

Everything opens at once.

  • The founder peer group
  • Founder introductions in your space
  • Matching with the right mentors
  • The founder platform — resources, playbooks, tools
  • Q&A calls
  • The Venture Assessment
  • One-to-one calls with the team and Phil
4

Each cohort runs in three phases

Assess, execute, decide. How deep you go is your call. Run the Venture Assessment, get direct feedback on the biggest things to tackle, and work out whether it makes sense to raise now and from whom, or whether it is too early and you should keep building.

5

Then stay on

This is not an accelerator and there is no graduation. Keep using the tracking tools and the community, be proactive, and engage.

The pivotal moment for you and your business

The Venture Assessment

Real, in-depth feedback from people who have done it.

Have you ever had a seasoned group of exited entrepreneurs look at your venture and tell you how they actually feel about it?

The Venture Assessment is a procedure we developed in our studios in Europe. We take you through an eleven-point questionnaire that mirrors the real state of the business, run it against years of operating experience using AI, and then the whole team chips in with feedback for you to execute on.

Afterwards it is yours. Share it with your team, build towards it, and come back to it as the business moves.

Value drivers of the community

Ongoing conversations with peers, mentors and investors — there when you need it most

Our Q&A calls bring in mentors regularly — some of the best operators in South Africa and internationally — to share what they learned, so you do not have to make the same mistakes they did.

Everybody chips in, the experts in the room give their feedback, and what comes out of it goes back into the Unleash brain: the shared set of best practices the whole community works from. Two founders each session also take the hot seat — thirty minutes each, one live problem, solved with the room.
