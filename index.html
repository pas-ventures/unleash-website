Meet
Phil

Exited founder & investor

The ecosystem doesn't need more advice. It needs people who have done it.

Philipp Ströhemann first landed in Cape Town in 2014 for a business school exchange, and never really left. He co-founded his first company with a South African partner that same year.

Over 15 years, Phil has built and scaled multiple businesses across B2B SaaS, marketplaces and tech-enabled services, from founding to exit: bootstrapping, building sales teams, scaling revenue, and selling to private equity.

Now he's putting that experience, and his own capital, behind the next generation of South African founders. He is not a fund manager, but someone who works alongside you on:

  • Getting the right idea
  • Finding the right target group
  • Building products and services people want to use and pay for
  • Finding the right co-founder
  • Building the team and scaling

What we believe
01

Community, not a fund

A peer group of operators who have built real companies, who show up when it matters.

Experience, not advice

Funded for SA reality

Funded for South African reality and flexibility, not shoehorned into Silicon Valley playbooks.
